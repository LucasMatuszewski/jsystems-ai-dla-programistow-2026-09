## Use in the PoC

Use the complete linked section as the complaint scenario's policy context, not this reference alone. It covers complaint submission, remedies, notification time, the complaint process, transport responsibilities and seller response time. Preserve qualifications, tables and links.

Apply the Polish marketplace provisions only. Determine whether the supplied facts establish the applicability of the consumer procedure. Do not infer a defect's cause, buyer responsibility or entitlement to a specific remedy from a photograph alone. Unknown circumstances or missing applicable conditions require clarification or human review.

Do not load the separate return policy into a complaint conversation. If the user wants to switch scenarios, direct them to a new case.

This is a dated snapshot, not an automatic policy-update service. Linked external pages and offer-specific terms have not been downloaded recursively. The source does not make the PoC a seller, Allegro representative or formal complaint-submission channel.
